Gastroenterologist with expertise in interventional endoscopy, hepatology, and digestive oncology.

Proven track record in clinical practice, medical education, and leadership roles. Has completed advanced training in Europe, Turkey, Egypt, and India. Primarily focused on interventional endoscopy and passionate about simulation-based training for medical education.
